Early access

Quarter is pre-launch. Early access is open.

We work closely with a few people shipping paid apps, especially apps built with AI tools.

What to expect

  • A conversation about what your app sells and where your users pay
  • A test project and test API keys
  • Help connecting Stripe, the App Store or Google Play
  • Help wiring access checks and credits into your app, or into your AI tool

Best fit: an app that sells a plan or credits, where a paying user has ever been locked out.

Where do your users pay?

We use these details only to reply to your request.

What you get

From a payment to the right access.

  1. A user pays

    Your app sells a plan or credits through Stripe Checkout, the App Store or Google Play.

  2. Quarter checks the store

    It verifies what the store sent, then asks the store for the latest state. Nothing unlocks unless the store confirms it.

  3. Access changes once

    The plan unlocks or the credits are added. The same payment seen twice changes nothing.

  4. Your app asks

    One call answers what this user has right now. Or read it from a table in your own database.

  5. Access follows the plan

    Renewals extend it. A cancel, a refund or a failed renewal takes it away at the right time.
