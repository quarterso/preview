Early access

Quarter is pre-launch. Early access is open.

We work closely with a few teams whose code writes to payment, email, commerce and CRM APIs.

What to expect

  • A conversation about the writes that worry you most
  • An ActionLint scan and a shadow audit, before Quarter sends anything
  • Help protecting the two to five writes that matter most
  • Your provider keys stay in your network from day one

Best fit: a duplicate or missing refund, stock change or CRM write costs you real money.

How teams start

Evidence first, protection second.

  1. 1

    Scan

    ActionLint finds the writes in your code a retry could repeat. Free.

  2. 2

    Shadow audit

    Shadow mode watches real traffic and reports what went wrong, without changing any call.

  3. 3

    Review

    We match what it found to real incidents and support tickets.

  4. 4

    Protected pilot

    Two to five writes that matter run through Quarter, with your keys in your network.

  5. 5

    Expand

    More actions and providers, as each one is proven.
