Try it

Five things to do in this demo, in any order.

  1. Load a sample payment run and watch Quarter check each payment.
  2. Decide the held payments. The large one needs two people, so switch to Sam for the second approval. Then release the file.
  3. Bayline Industrial Supply changed its bank details 2 days ago. Confirm them with a call-back or a link to the vendor.
  4. Check an account before you pay it, such as one other businesses reported.
  5. Change what a check does, then load another run to see the difference.
