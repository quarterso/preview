Held payments

Quarter checks your open NetSuite bills every 15 minutes. A clear bill comes off Payment Hold. Anything else stays held until a person decides here, and Quarter writes the result back to the bill.

Try it

Four things to do with the sample NetSuite account.

  1. Sync from NetSuite. Quarter checks the open bills and takes the clear ones off Payment Hold.
  2. Decide the held bills. Alder Packaging is over $50,000, so it needs two people: approve as Dana, then switch to Sam.
  3. Change the amount of a cleared bill in NetSuite, then sync. Quarter checks it again.
  4. Clear a hold Quarter put on a bill in NetSuite, then sync. Quarter puts it back and tells you.

7 open bills wait on Payment Hold in the sample NetSuite account. Your NetSuite workflow holds each new bill until Quarter has checked it. Here you sync by hand.
