For banks

Payment controls for your business customers, before the file reaches you.

Quarter checks the vendor payments your business customers send by ACH, check and wire, and holds anything suspicious for a named person at the business. It writes your Positive Pay layout, keeps the record of every decision, and never touches the money.

Why now

Your originators are asked for a fraud process. Many have never written one down.

Every originator needs a process

Since June 19, 2026, Nacha's rules ask every business that originates ACH, of any size, for risk-based procedures to identify payments made under false pretenses, reviewed at least once a year. The rule does not require checks before processing.

Source: Nacha Operating Rules, fraud monitoring phase 2

Your program can consider theirs

An ODFI's own procedures may take into account the procedures of others in the origination of entries, when that reliance is reasonable and clear. A business customer that can show its controls makes that easier.

Source: Nacha Operating Rules, fraud monitoring phase 1

Positive Pay is underused

About 65% of banks and credit unions have fewer than half of their treasury clients using Positive Pay. Quarter writes the issue file in your layout, so a customer has one less reason not to.

Source: Datos Insights, cited by Alkami, Check Positive Pay: Banks and Credit Unions’ Best Fraud Defense, March 2025

What your customers get

The controls a careful controller would run, on every payment.

Checks before release

Every payment in an ACH run, a check run or a single wire is checked before your customer sends it to you. With NetSuite connected, every open vendor bill is checked before it is paid. Anything that fails a check waits for a named person.

Bank detail change control

A changed vendor account holds every payment to it until it is confirmed: by the vendor logging in to its own bank, or by a call-back to a number your customer already had. Quarter records who called, which number and where it came from.

Two-person approval

At or above your customer’s threshold, approving a payment takes two different people, and so do smaller payments to one payee that add up to it in 7 days. Nobody approves a payment to a vendor whose bank details they changed in the last 90 days.

Check runs and Positive Pay

Quarter screens the check register before the checks are printed, then writes the Positive Pay issue file in a generic CSV, Jack Henry Banno, Citizens Bank or F&M Bank layout, or a bank’s own delimited or fixed-width layout that an admin enters from the bank’s file specification. Your customer uploads the file to you as it does today.

A written procedure and its evidence

Nacha's fraud-monitoring rule asks your ACH originators for a documented, risk-based process, reviewed every year. Quarter writes your customer's procedure from its own settings, records each yearly review and keeps the evidence behind every decision. It helps your customer show a process. It does not make anyone compliant.

Your vendor review

What your third-party risk team will want, and where it is.

  • Security: how data is sealed, who can decide, incident notice within 4 hours for banks
  • Terms of service (draft): no guarantee, a 12-month fee cap, never moves money
  • Data processing agreement (draft)
  • Subprocessors: every outside service that handles customer data

A written information security program, policies, and CAIQ and SIG Lite answers are written and will be shared once Quarter opens.

Not in place yet

  • A SOC 2 report or an outside penetration test
  • A certificate of insurance and financial statements
  • Terms in effect. They are drafts until the company is formed
  • Any customer or bank reference. Quarter is pre-launch

Once Quarter opens

How a business customer would start.

  1. Your customer opens its own account

    In its own name, once Quarter opens. Nothing is shared with the bank by default.

  2. Quarter checks the business

    Before it switches the account to live payments.

  3. Your customer sends you files as today

    The released payment file and the Positive Pay file, from its own bank portal.

What to know first

  • Quarter never receives, holds or sends money, and never connects to your systems. Your customer sends every file to you.
  • It adds to your own controls, such as Positive Pay and payee name checks. It does not replace them.
  • Quarter is pre-launch. No bank refers customers to it yet, and it names no bank as a partner.
  • Quarter pays no referral fees.
