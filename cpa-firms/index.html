Vendor payment controls your clients can show you.

Your client's payments are checked before they leave its bank, and you can read who changed bank details, who confirmed them and who approved.

01Clients

The controls you would recommend, running on every payment.

  1. 01

    A check before every vendor payment

    Open NetSuite bills, ACH files, check registers and single wires, checked before they reach the bank. Risky payments wait for a named person at your client.

  2. 02

    Bank changes confirmed, not assumed

    A changed account holds payment until the vendor logs in to its own bank, or someone calls a number the client had before the change.

  3. 03

    Two people, and the right two

    Two different approvers above the client’s threshold, and for smaller payments to one payee that add up to it. Nobody approves a payment to a vendor whose bank details they changed.

02Reports

Records you can read, instead of a spreadsheet you have to trust.

R.1The vendor changes report
Every change to vendors and bank details in a period, with who made it and how it was confirmed.
R.2The audit log
Every action, person and piece of evidence, each entry chained by its hash to the one before.
R.3The controls report
The client’s controls mapped to Nacha’s 2026 fraud-monitoring rules, with a year of counts and the yearly review dates.
R.4The monthly summary
What was held, what each hold turned out to be, and any payment that left and later proved wrong.

03Working together

How a firm would work with a client, once Quarter opens.

  1. 1

    The client opens its own account

    In its own name, once Quarter opens, with a trial or a 60-day pilot.

  2. 2

    The client invites you

    As a member of its own Quarter organization, with the role it chooses. A viewer reads every report; an approver can also download claim files.

What to know first

  • One view of all your clients in one place is planned, not built. Today each client invites you to its own organization.
  • On the Team plan, you count toward the client’s 5 members.
  • Quarter pays no referral fees or commissions.
  • Quarter does not make a client compliant with any rule, and its reports are not an opinion on the client’s controls. That stays your work.
  • Quarter is pre-launch, with no customers yet.
