01Statement

What Quarter is for

Most payment fraud is not hacking. It is one email that changes a vendor's bank details, and a run that goes out on Friday because it is Friday.

Quarter sits between the bill and the bank. It checks every payment before it leaves, against rules written down in plain words.

A risky payment waits for a named person. A large one waits for two. The rest go out the way they do today.

Quarter never touches the money and decides nothing alone. It keeps the record of who decided, and why.

One payment, start to finish. An example.

  1. checkedA payment run goes in. Every payment is checked before it leaves.
  2. heldThe bank details changed recently and nobody confirmed them with the vendor.
  3. verifiedThe vendor logs in to their own bank, and the account matches.
  4. releasedThe payment goes out with the run. The record keeps who decided, and why.

03Method

Four steps between the bill and the bank.

Step by step
  1. 1

    Check

    Every payment is checked against 33 rules, each with its reason in plain words.

  2. 2

    Hold

    Anything risky waits for a named person. Large payments need two.

  3. 3

    Release

    Cleared payments go out the way they do today. Held bills stay on Payment Hold.

  4. 4

    Reconcile

    Who checked, who approved and the evidence, kept as one record.

4.1 Checks

33 checks on every payment, in this order. Each one holds or warns by default; 6 cannot be turned off.

  1. Payee is not a vendorhold
  2. Vendor is blockedhold, always
  3. Bank details never confirmedhold
  4. Bank details changed recentlyhold
  5. Account is not the one on filehold
  6. Payee name does not matchwarn
  7. First payment to this vendorwarn
  8. Unusually large amountwarn
  9. Duplicate paymenthold
  10. Account shared with another vendorhold
  11. Reported as fraud on Quarterwarn
  12. Resembles a sanctioned partyhold, always
  13. Invalid routing numberhold
  14. IBAN fails its check digitshold
  15. Check number already usedhold
  16. Payee line differs from the vendor namewarn
  17. Just under the two-person thresholdwarn
  18. Account belongs to an employeehold
  19. Payroll to an account not on the employee listhold, always
  20. Dormant vendor with new bank detailswarn
  21. Split to stay under the two-person thresholdwarn

4.3 Decisions

Hold, warn or clear. A held payment waits for a named person; large payments need two different people. Every decision carries a reason.

4.4 Kept
  • Every payment run, and every finding on every payment in it
  • Every decision, with who made it, when, and the reason they gave
  • Every piece of verification evidence: email codes, bank confirmations and call-backs
  • Every change to a vendor or to bank details, with the history kept

Account numbers are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Outside the files you release, only the last 4 digits show.

4.5 Never
  • Hold your money, or move it
  • Ask anyone for a bank password
  • Show a full account number outside the files you release
  • Let anyone, including Quarter, rewrite the record unnoticed
