Payment controls for the internet

Every payment, under your rules.

Any business or software that sends money checks it with Quarter first. Risky payments wait for a person. The rest go out as they do today.

Try the demo Read the docs

The fraud

One email changes a vendor's bank details. Friday's run pays a stranger.

Quarter holds every payment to a changed account until the vendor confirms it from their own bank, or you record a call-back to a number you already had.

Example: one payment, start to finish

  1. checkedA payment run goes in. Every payment is checked before it leaves.
  2. heldThe bank details changed recently and nobody confirmed them with the vendor.
  3. verifiedThe vendor logs in to their own bank, and the account matches.
  4. releasedThe payment goes out with the run. The record keeps who decided, and why.

How it works

Four steps between the bill and the bank.

  1. 01

    Check

    Every payment is checked against 32 rules, each with its reason in plain words.

  2. 02

    Hold

    Anything risky waits for a named person. Large payments need two.

  3. 03

    Release

    Cleared payments go out the way they do today. Held bills stay on Payment Hold.

  4. 04

    Reconcile

    Who checked, who approved and the evidence, kept as one record.
