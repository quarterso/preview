Subprocessors

The draft list of companies that run parts of Quarter and may handle customer data, with what each one does.

Draft, not yet in effect. It applies to no one until the company that runs Quarter exists and publishes this version with an effective date.

Effective date: [Effective date]

  1. Not subprocessors

These companies run parts of Quarter and may handle customer data, each under a written contract. Quarter tells customer admins at least 30 days before a new one starts, so a customer can object.

CompanyWhat it does for QuarterWhat it handles
DigitalOceanHosts the api, the background worker, the console and the PostgreSQL database, in its New York region.All customer data, with account numbers and payment files sealed.
ResendSends Quarter's email: sign-in links, invitations, vendor verification links and codes, and notices.Email addresses, names and the content of those emails. Never an account number.
PlaidLets a vendor confirm its bank details by logging in to its bank, only when the vendor chooses to.The vendor's bank login, which goes to the bank, and the account details and owner's name the bank returns. Quarter keeps only the routing numbers, the last 4 digits and whether they match.
AnthropicWrites case briefs with its model, Claude, only for an organization whose admin turned case briefs on, and only when someone asks for a brief.The fields listed for case briefs: payee and vendor names, amounts, the last 4 digits of accounts, check findings, and email senders, subjects, dates and authentication results. Never a full account or routing number, a phone number or an email body. Nothing is sent while briefs are off.
StripeBills Quarter's own subscriptions.The billing contact and subscription. Card details go to Stripe and never reach Quarter.
CloudflareRuns Quarter's DNS, hosts this website, forwards email sent to Quarter's addresses to Quarter's inbox, and stores Quarter's nightly off-site database copy.The IP address and request details of website visitors, the email it forwards, and the off-site database copies, which are encrypted before they reach it.
GitHubHolds Quarter's source code and runs its scheduled jobs: the OFAC sanctions list download and the nightly off-site backup.During the backup, the database passes through GitHub's runner, which encrypts the copy before it is stored. The sanctions job uses only public data.

01Not subprocessors

NetSuite is the customer's own system. The customer connects it. On the customer's behalf, Quarter reads vendors and open vendor bills from it and writes its result onto each bill using NetSuite's Payment Hold. It is not a subprocessor.

The customer's bank receives the files the customer sends it. Quarter never sends anything to a bank.
