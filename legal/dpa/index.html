Data processing summary

Draft, not yet in effect. This is a draft for review by counsel. It does not apply to anyone until Quarter publishes a final version with an effective date.

Effective date: [Effective date]

This is a summary of the data processing agreement between [Legal entity name] ("Quarter") and its customers. The full agreement is available on request once it is in effect.

Roles

The customer controls the personal data it puts into Quarter. Quarter processes it as the customer's service provider, or processor.

Subject matter

Checking the payments the customer plans to send, and its open NetSuite vendor bills when it connects NetSuite, before they are paid, and keeping the record of those checks.

The data and the people

  • Vendors and their contacts: names, contact emails, bank routing numbers, sealed account numbers, and the evidence of each confirmation, including the account owner's name and bank returned by Plaid.
  • Employees: a keyed fingerprint of each employee's bank account and the customer's own reference, never the account number.
  • Payees in payment files and check registers: names, amounts and bank details.
  • The customer's people: names, work emails, roles and their actions in the audit log.

Instructions

Quarter processes the data only to provide the service and on the customer's instructions, which are these terms, the agreement and the settings the customer chooses. The network uses keyed fingerprints and counts for fraud prevention across customers and never shares who reported.

Confidentiality

Everyone at Quarter who can reach customer data is bound to keep it confidential.

Security measures in place

  • Account numbers, payment files and integration credentials sealed with AES-256-GCM.
  • Row-level security in PostgreSQL that keeps each customer's rows apart.
  • API keys, sign-in links, verification links and email codes stored only as hashes.
  • Sign-in links that work once, for 15 minutes, and sessions of 12 hours.
  • A passkey, as well as the sign-in link, for approvers and admins once payments are live, checked again before each money decision.
  • An audit log the application can only add to while the account is open.

Subprocessors

Quarter uses the subprocessors listed here. It tells customer admins before a new subprocessor starts handling customer data, so a customer can object.

Breach notice

If a breach affects customer data, Quarter tells the customer without undue delay, with what it knows and what it is doing.

Deletion at the end

When a customer closes its account, it can first download an export of its records. Quarter deletes the data 30 days after closing, and host backups that still hold it age out within 7 days after that.

Audits

Quarter answers a customer's security questionnaire once a year, and again after a breach or when a regulator asks.
