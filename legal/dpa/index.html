Data processing agreement

The draft data processing agreement: roles, business purposes, what Quarter may not do, security, subprocessors, requests, incidents, audits and deletion.

This agreement is between [Legal entity name] ("Quarter") and the customer that accepts the terms of service. It is part of those terms and covers the personal information Quarter processes for the customer. It is written to meet the California Consumer Privacy Act and its regulations (including 11 CCR 7051) and the processor terms of other US state privacy laws.

011. Roles and scope

The customer decides what personal information it puts into Quarter and why. Quarter processes it as the customer's service provider (CCPA) or processor (other state laws). The data and the people are listed in the annex below.

022. Business purposes

Quarter processes the personal information only for these business purposes:

  • checking the payments the customer plans to send, and its open NetSuite vendor bills when it connects NetSuite, before they are paid;
  • confirming vendors' bank details at the customer's request, by email code, call-back record or the vendor's own bank login;
  • keeping the record of those checks, decisions and confirmations for the customer, and returning it on request;
  • security and integrity: detecting and resisting payment fraud, including the network, which uses keyed fingerprints of business accounts and shares only counts, never who reported, as the CCPA allows for this purpose (Cal. Civ. Code 1798.140(e)(2); 11 CCR 7050(a)(4));
  • keeping the service working, secure and correct.

033. Instructions

The customer's instructions are the terms, this agreement and the settings its admins choose. Quarter tells the customer if it believes an instruction breaks the law.

044. What Quarter may not do

  • sell or share the personal information, as the CCPA defines those words;
  • retain, use or disclose it for any purpose other than the business purposes above, including any other commercial purpose;
  • retain, use or disclose it outside the direct business relationship with the customer, except the network's counts under section 2;
  • combine it with personal information from other sources, except as the CCPA's regulations allow for the business purposes above.

Quarter may make de-identified data from the personal information, as the CCPA defines it (Cal. Civ. Code 1798.140(m)), to run and improve its checks. Quarter takes reasonable measures so the data cannot be associated with a person or household, publicly commits to keep it that way and not to try to re-identify it, and binds anyone who receives it to the same. De-identified data is not personal information under this agreement.

Quarter complies with the CCPA and gives the personal information the same level of privacy protection the CCPA requires of the customer. Quarter tells the customer within 5 business days if it decides it can no longer meet its obligations here. The customer may then, on notice, take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate any use that is not allowed, including suspending the processing.

055. Confidentiality and security

Everyone at Quarter who can reach customer data is bound to keep it confidential. Quarter keeps a written information security program with these measures, among others:

  • account numbers, payment files and integration credentials sealed with AES-256-GCM;
  • row-level security in PostgreSQL that keeps each customer's rows apart;
  • API keys, sign-in links, verification links and email codes stored only as hashes;
  • sign-in links that work once, for 15 minutes, and sessions of 12 hours;
  • a passkey, as well as the sign-in link, for approvers and admins once payments are live, checked again before each money decision;
  • an audit log the application can only add to while the account is open.

066. Subprocessors

Quarter uses the subprocessors listed here. Each is bound by a written contract to terms that protect the data at least as well as this agreement. Quarter tells customer admins at least 30 days before a new subprocessor starts handling customer data. The customer may object on reasonable grounds; if the parties cannot resolve it, the customer may cancel and receive a refund of any prepaid fees for the unused period.

077. Requests from people

Quarter helps the customer answer requests to know, delete or correct personal information. An admin can find, export and erase a contact's details in the console, and Quarter passes on any request it receives directly. Payment records the customer keeps for its retention period stay unchanged. A correction never rewrites evidence of a past contact, which is accurate as a record of what happened; the console gives the reason for the customer's answer.

088. Incidents

Quarter tells the customer within 24 hours of determining that a security incident put its personal information at risk, with what it knows, and gives a written account within 72 hours. Quarter helps the customer meet its own notice duties to people and regulators.

099. Showing compliance

Quarter answers the customer's security questionnaire once a year, and again after an incident or when a regulator asks. Where a regulator of the customer or its bank has the right to examine Quarter's service, Quarter cooperates. Quarter makes available the information needed to show it meets this agreement, including its policies and any third-party audit report it holds. The customer may take reasonable and appropriate steps to make sure Quarter uses the personal information as this agreement allows, including an audit by an independent auditor on 30 days' notice, at the customer's cost, once a year.

1010. Retention, return and deletion

Quarter keeps payment files for 90 days after they are scanned or released, and other customer data while the account is open. When the customer closes its account, it can first download an export of its records. Quarter deletes the data 30 days after closing. Host backups that still hold it age out within 7 days of its deletion.

If the organization sent live payments, Quarter keeps a sealed copy of its payment evidence until its evidence period ends: 7 years from closing, or longer if the customer chose up to 30 years. The customer instructs this so it can show its bank, auditor or insurer what was checked and decided. Quarter may also use the copy to establish, exercise or defend legal claims about the service. The copy holds only the last 4 digits of account numbers. Only Quarter staff can open it, to return it to the customer or for a legal claim. Quarter deletes it when the period ends. The customer gives this instruction by accepting this agreement.

1111. Other state laws

Where another US state privacy law applies, such as those of Virginia, Colorado or Connecticut, Quarter also processes the data only on documented instructions, keeps it confidential, deletes or returns it at the end of the service as section 10 says, makes available what the customer needs to show compliance, and binds its subprocessors to the same duties.

1212. Term and precedence

This agreement lasts as long as Quarter processes personal information for the customer. Where it and the terms differ on personal information, this agreement wins.

13Annex: the data and the people

  • Vendors and their contacts: names, contact emails, bank routing numbers, sealed account numbers, and the evidence of each confirmation, including the call-back record and whether a bank login matched.
  • Employees: a keyed fingerprint of each employee's bank account and the customer's own reference, never the account number.
  • Payees in payment files and check registers: names, amounts and bank details.
  • The customer's people: names, work emails, roles and their actions in the audit log.
  • Frequency: whenever the customer checks payments or confirms vendors, for as long as the account is open.
