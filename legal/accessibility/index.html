Accessibility

The standard Quarter builds its website, console and vendor confirmation page to, what is not yet checked, and how to report a barrier.

Draft, not yet in effect. It applies to no one until the company that runs Quarter exists and publishes this version with an effective date.

  1. Where things stand
  2. Report a barrier

Quarter builds its website, its console and the page vendors use to confirm their bank details to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

01Where things stand

No independent audit has checked Quarter against WCAG 2.2 yet, so Quarter does not claim to conform. The vendor confirmation page comes first, because the people who use it did not choose Quarter.

The website's moving background has a Pause motion button.

02Report a barrier

If something on the website, the console or a vendor page is hard or impossible to use with your assistive technology, write to [Accessibility contact address] with the page and what happened. Quarter replies, helps you finish what you were doing another way, and fixes the barrier.
