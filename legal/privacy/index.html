This notice explains what [Legal entity name] ("Quarter", "we") collects about people, what we do with it, and the choices you have.

01Our two roles

For the data a business puts into Quarter about its vendors, employees and payments, Quarter is that business's service provider, or processor. The business decides what goes in, and we act on its instructions under the data processing agreement.

For the accounts of people who sign in to Quarter, and for this website, Quarter decides what is collected.

02People who use the console

Your name, work email, role and organization, the times you sign in, and your IP address in our server logs. We use them to sign you in, to show who did what in the audit log, to send you notices, and to keep the service safe.

For each passkey you add, its public key, the name you give it and when it was last used. Your fingerprint, face or device PIN never leaves your device, and Quarter never receives it.

03Vendor contacts and bank details

A customer gives us its vendors' names, contact emails, and bank routing and account numbers, directly or through its NetSuite connection. Quarter seals each account number with AES-256-GCM as it arrives and shows only the last 4 digits, except in the payment files the customer releases to send to its bank.

When a vendor confirms its bank details, we keep the evidence: whether an emailed code was entered, the record of a call-back (who called, which number, where the number came from, and the result), and, when the vendor logs in to its bank through Plaid, whether the account matches and how closely the account owner's name matches.

Plaid connects the vendor to its bank, under Plaid's own end user privacy policy, which Plaid shows before the vendor logs in. The vendor's bank password goes to the bank through Plaid, never to Quarter. Quarter does not keep the full account numbers, the owner's names or Plaid's access token from that login, and the result is shown only to the vendor and the customer who asked.

04Employee data

A customer can give Quarter its employee account list, so payments to an employee's own account are held. The payroll file is read in the browser. Quarter keeps only a keyed fingerprint of each employee's bank account and the customer's own reference for the employee, never the account number.

05Payment files

Payment files and check registers hold payee names, amounts and bank details. Quarter seals each file when it arrives and deletes it 90 days after it was scanned or released. The results of the checks and the decisions people made are kept as payment evidence.

When a customer connects NetSuite, Quarter reads each open vendor bill, with its vendor, number and amount, and keeps the result of each check.

06This website

This website has no forms and asks for no personal details. Nothing you type into the demo leaves your browser.

07Cookies, tracking and Do Not Track

This website sets no cookies and runs no advertising or analytics scripts. The demo and the docs remember a few choices in your own browser, and nothing from them is sent to Quarter.

The website does not track you over time or across other sites, and lets no one else do so. A browser's Do Not Track signal changes nothing, because there is no tracking to turn off.

The console uses one cookie, to keep you signed in.

08Who we share it with

We share data only with the subprocessors that run parts of the service, with the customer the data belongs to, and with authorities when the law requires it. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

Quarter may use de-identified data about how its checks perform, such as how often a check held a payment that turned out to be fraud, to improve the checks for every customer. We keep it de-identified, never try to re-identify it, and bind anyone who receives it to do the same.

The network shares counts, never names, and only for business accounts. A fingerprint of a business account a customer confirmed or reported counts toward a number other customers see, for 24 months. Who confirmed or reported is never shared. Accounts that belong to a person never enter it. A business that believes a report about its account is wrong can write to [Legal contact address]; Quarter keeps the dispute, its routing number and last 4 digits, and its decision, but not the account number.

09How long we keep it

  • Payment files: 90 days after they are scanned or released.
  • Payment evidence (payment runs, decisions, vendor verifications, call-back records and the audit log): while the organization is open, then as a sealed copy until its evidence period ends, as the next section explains.
  • Console accounts, vendors and employee fingerprints: while the organization is open, and 30 days after it closes. The sealed copy kept after closing also holds the console accounts, the vendors and the employee references, but no fingerprints.
  • Network counts: 24 months from the last contribution.
  • Server logs, which hold IP addresses: Quarter keeps no copy. Its host keeps them for a short time for troubleshooting.

10Closing an account

An admin can download an export of the organization's records and close the account from the console. Closing ends every session and API key at once. Quarter deletes the organization's data 30 days later. Host backups that still hold it age out within 7 days of its deletion.

If the organization sent live payments, Quarter keeps a sealed copy of its payment evidence until its evidence period ends: 7 years from closing, or longer if the customer chose up to 30 years. The customer instructs this so it can show its bank, auditor or insurer what was checked and decided. Quarter may also use the copy to establish, exercise or defend legal claims about the service. The copy holds only the last 4 digits of account numbers. Only Quarter staff can open it, to return it to the customer or for a legal claim. Quarter deletes it when the period ends.

11How we protect it

  • Account numbers, payment files and integration credentials are sealed with AES-256-GCM.
  • PostgreSQL row-level security keeps each customer's rows apart, on top of the checks in the code.
  • API keys, sign-in links, verification links and email codes are stored only as hashes.
  • A sign-in link works once, for 15 minutes. A session lasts 12 hours.
  • Once payments are live, approvers and admins also sign in with a passkey, and use it again before approving, releasing or changing settings.

12Your rights

Depending on where you live, US state privacy laws may give you the right to know what we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, and to have it deleted. California's law also covers sole proprietors who are paid as vendors. We answer these requests whether or not a law requires us to.

Send a request to [Privacy contact address]. We check that the request comes from you before we act on it. Someone you authorize can ask for you. We never treat you differently for asking.

If your data is in Quarter because a business you work with or are paid by put it there, we pass your request to that business and help it answer.

Two limits apply. Payment records a business must keep stay for their retention period, even after a request to delete. And a correction never rewrites evidence of a past contact, such as the number a call-back dialled or the address a code was sent to: that record is accurate as a record of what happened. You can still ask for your details in it to be deleted.

13Where the data is

Quarter's servers and database are in the United States, in its host's New York region.

14Children

Quarter is for businesses and is not meant for children.

15Changes and contact

If we change this notice, we update it here with a new effective date and tell customer admins by email.
