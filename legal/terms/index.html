Who can use Quarter, and how you accept

Quarter is for businesses in the United States. It is not for personal or household use.

An admin accepts these terms for the business when asking for live mode in the console, or the business signs an order that refers to them. The person who accepts confirms they may bind the business. Test mode, which holds only made-up data, is available before that.

What Quarter does

Quarter checks the payments your business plans to send, before you send them to your bank. It reads payment files, check registers and single payments, matches each payment to your vendors, and holds anything that fails a check for a person at your business to decide.

It returns the checked payment file with the held payments removed, and a Positive Pay file for check runs. You send these to your bank. Each released file comes with its SHA-256, and Quarter can tell you whether a file is the one it released.

Quarter keeps a record of each check, decision and piece of evidence, and writes a fraud-monitoring procedure from your settings.

Quarter never moves money

Quarter never receives, holds or sends money. It never pays anyone for you, never debits anyone, and never sends files or instructions to your bank.

You send each file to your own bank, and you decide every payment. A released file holds only the payments your people let through.

Your decisions, and no guarantee

Quarter does not guarantee that it will detect any fraudulent, mistaken or sanctioned payment. A payment that passes every check may still be wrong.

You remain solely responsible for each payment you release. Quarter does not repay losses from any payment, and it offers no loss reimbursement, insurance or guarantee of any kind.

Quarter helps you run and record a fraud-monitoring process. It does not make you compliant with any law or rule, including the Nacha Operating Rules and sanctions law, and nothing Quarter shows you is legal advice.

Holds and delays

Holding a payment for a person to decide is how Quarter works. A hold, a finding or a network count says what Quarter saw. It is not a finding that a vendor, employee or other payee did anything wrong, and it is not a reason on its own to refuse a payment you owe.

A person at your business can decide any held payment at any time, and your admins choose which checks hold. You stay responsible for paying your vendors, employees and taxes on time and for your payment deadlines, including when a payment is held or Quarter is unavailable.

When Quarter is unavailable

Quarter does not promise any level of availability. Quarter is never the only way to send a payment file: if it is unavailable, you can send a file to your bank yourself, under your own controls.

A NetSuite bill Quarter holds stays held until a person decides it. If you cannot wait, your NetSuite administrator can clear NetSuite's Payment Hold on the bill. Quarter records that it was cleared outside Quarter when it next reads the bill, so your records show it. Payments made that way were not checked by Quarter.

Quarter emails your admins when an outage stops your people from deciding held payments, and again when it ends, with what happened.

The files you send

Before you send a released file, check that its number of payments and total match the release in the console. After you download it, keeping it unchanged until it reaches your bank is your responsibility. You can compare it with the SHA-256 Quarter gave, or ask Quarter whether it is the file it released. Quarter is not responsible for a file changed after release.

The Nacha Operating Rules

When Quarter returns a checked ACH file, it performs that function for you as a Third-Party Service Provider under the Nacha Operating Rules. It keeps a written, risk-based fraud-monitoring process for that role and reviews it every year.

You remain the Originator. Your agreement with your bank, and your duties under the Rules, stay yours.

Sanctions

Quarter screens payee names against OFAC's sanctions lists and holds a possible match for you to decide. Quarter screens names against OFAC's lists. It cannot see who owns a payee, so it does not find entities owned 50% or more by listed persons, which OFAC's 50 Percent Rule also blocks. It does not transliterate, so a live payment to a name written in another script is held as not screened. The duty not to pay a sanctioned party stays with you and your bank.

You confirm that your business is not a sanctioned person, is not owned 50% or more by one, does not act for one, and is not in a country or region under comprehensive US sanctions. Quarter may refuse or suspend service where sanctions law requires it.

Systems you connect

When you connect NetSuite, you authorize Quarter to act for you with the credentials you create. Quarter reads your vendors and open vendor bills, and, with the payment flow on, writes its result onto each bill using NetSuite's Payment Hold and Quarter's own field. It writes nothing else.

You keep your agreement with the provider of each system you connect, and you may disconnect it at any time.

Vendor confirmations

You may send your vendors a link to confirm their bank details. You send links only to your own vendors, for payments you plan to make.

A vendor may choose to log in to its own bank through Plaid. The result is shown only to you and the vendor, is never used in the network, and is used only under Plaid's terms.

Trials, plans, billing and renewal

  • When Quarter switches on live mode, the organization gets a 14-day free trial of the Team plan. A trial never turns into a paid plan by itself.
  • When the trial ends, a live organization without a subscription becomes inactive. People can still sign in, read its records and finish runs already scanned. Quarter refuses new vendors, new payment runs and new payment checks until a subscription starts.
  • Test mode is free for developers, with small caps on what a test project can hold.
  • Your price is the one in your order, or the one shown when you subscribe.
  • A subscription renews automatically at the end of each month or year, at the price then in effect, until you cancel. An admin can cancel at any time under Manage billing in the console. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the plan runs to the end of the period already paid, and paid periods are not refunded.
  • For a yearly plan, Quarter emails your admins 30 days before it renews, with the date and the amount.
  • Quarter tells your admins by email at least 30 days before a price change applies to your next renewal.
  • Subscriptions are billed through Stripe. Fees are due as invoiced and exclude taxes, which you pay.

A count reports what customers have told Quarter. Quarter does not verify every report and does not guarantee that any count is accurate. You make your own payment decision.

You warrant that each fraud report you file is accurate and made in good faith, and you indemnify Quarter against claims arising from any report that is not.

A business that believes a report about its account is wrong can write to Quarter at [Legal contact address]. While Quarter reviews it, the report still counts, and an account check shows that it is disputed. Quarter may ask you for the facts behind your report, and you agree to answer within 10 business days. If Quarter decides the report is wrong, it stops counting reports made before that decision. Your own record of the report is kept.

Closing your account

The customer instructs this so it can show its bank, auditor or insurer what was checked and decided. Quarter may also use the copy to establish, exercise or defend legal claims about the service. The copy holds only the last 4 digits of account numbers. Only Quarter staff can open it, to return it to the customer or for a legal claim. Quarter deletes it when the period ends.
