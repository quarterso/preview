The control layer for agents

Every paid call an agent makes goes through Quarter. It is checked before the money is spent, and recorded after.

Control

Rules that hold before a call leaves, not alerts after the bill.

  • Caps before spend

    One budget for everything, or limits per agent, task and customer. A call that would break one never reaches the vendor, even with many calls at once.

  • Approvals

    A call above your approval line waits for a person. Approve it once from the dashboard or the terminal, and the agent retries.

  • Agent keys

    Each agent calls with a Quarter key. Vendor keys pass straight through and are never stored.

  • Credits

    Give an agent a balance to spend from. Refill it by rule, within a monthly limit you set.

  • Every vendor

    Models priced by token, and search, scraping and data APIs priced per call. Any vendor with a base URL.

  • Every way agents pay

    x402, MPP, cards and invoices get the same caps and approvals. Your own wallet pays only what Quarter signed off.

  • Runaway stop

    An agent spending ten times faster than usual, or sending the same request over and over, is paused until a person lets it go on.

  • Cheaper model near the cap

    A call that would break a cap moves to a cheaper model you allowed, instead of failing.

  • Streaming

    Streamed answers pass straight through. Usage is read from the stream as it ends.

Visibility

One place to see what agents spend, and what you spend on them.

  • Live dashboard

    Spend this month, caps used, and approvals waiting, behind a finance password.

  • Cost per customer

    Tag each call with the customer it served and see what each one costs to run.

  • Spend on agents

    Record seats, subscriptions, compute and people against each agent, one-off or monthly.

  • Month-end journal

    One file with every line by customer and vendor, balanced to the cent, ready to import.

  • Reports

    Spend by agent, task, customer or vendor, on screen or as CSV.

  • Margin per customer

    Add what each customer paid and see which ones your agents lose money on.

  • Month-end forecast

    Where the month will land at the current rate, and the day each cap will be reached. Slack hears before it happens.

  • Bill your customers

    Invoice each customer for the agent work done for them, at cost or with your markup.

  • Teams and roles

    Admins, finance, approvers and viewers each see and do only their part. Every action names the person.

  • Books in QuickBooks

    The month-end journal posts straight to QuickBooks Online, once, never duplicated.

  • Approve in Slack

    Approve or deny from Slack. The agent waits for the answer, and the ledger records who decided.

  • Usage into Stripe

    Each customer's agent usage goes to Stripe billing daily, with your markup, never counted twice.

  • Signed approvals

    Every approval can be exported as signed proof of who allowed what, for auditors and payment partners.

  • Margin guard

    When a customer drops under your margin floor, Quarter alerts you, moves calls to a cheaper model, or stops them.

  • Audit pack

    One export per month for SOX and SOC 2: every entry, approval and cap in force, in a ledger where any edit shows.

  • Dispute evidence

    For any call: who allowed it, up to how much, what was charged, and the signed receipt.

  • Vendor bills matched

    Each vendor invoice is checked against what Quarter metered. Mismatches are flagged and unbilled usage is accrued.

  • Commitments tested

    See whether a provider commitment would have saved money, replayed on your own last months of usage.

  • Never undercounted

    If a vendor reports no usage, the full hold is charged. Spend is never missed.
