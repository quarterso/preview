02Starting

Two ways a company will start.

Neither is open yet. Both are built into the product.

The 14-day free trial

  1. Team plan, free for 14 days from the day Quarter switches an account to live payments, after it checks the business.
  2. Test mode with sample data is free and never ends.
  3. If the trial ends without a plan, the company can still read everything and decide runs already checked, but cannot add vendors or runs.

The 60-day paid pilot

  1. For companies with an AP team and a controller. It runs on real payment runs, and its cost counts toward the first year.
  2. A founder sets it up with the company: NetSuite or file upload, check settings, approvers with passkeys, and the bank’s Positive Pay layout.
  3. The first real run is checked together, with a weekly meeting and a summary at day 60 of what was checked, held and caught. It says what happened, nothing more.

03Terms

Support from two founders, and what is not included.

Support

  • Email to the founders. Real people, no ticket bot.
  • We answer as soon as we can on US business days. We make no response-time promise until we can measure and keep one.
  • Incident notices as written on the security page, within 24 hours for every customer.
  • Quarter is never in the path of the money. If it is down, you pay as you did before and keep a manual log.

Not included

  • Moving, holding or sending money, or sending any file to your bank. You send every file.
  • A guarantee or reimbursement of losses. You decide every payment.
  • Compliance. Quarter helps you run and show a fraud-monitoring process. It does not make you compliant, and it is not legal advice.
  • Holding wages paid to your employees' own accounts. Payroll to an account on your employee list warns instead.
  • Single sign-on, a SOC 2 report or an outside penetration test. Not yet.
  • Connectors other than NetSuite. Other systems upload a NACHA file, CSV or check register.
  • Phone support, 24/7 cover or a service level agreement.

04Questions

Questions

Why are there no prices?

Quarter is not open yet and bills nothing. Prices are not set. Everything else on this page is what each plan will include.

Does Quarter take a cut of our payments?

No. Quarter never holds or moves money, so it cannot take a cut of a payment. You pay for a plan.

Is there a guarantee against losses?

No. Quarter does not guarantee that it will catch every fraudulent payment, and you decide every payment you release. Its records are the evidence your bank, auditor or insurer will ask for.

Why price by vendors?

Your risk grows with the number of businesses you pay, not with how often you check. So you can check every run without paying more. An active vendor is one you paid in the last 12 months.

What about AP platforms and payroll providers?

The API is included in every plan for connecting your own systems. A plan for software that sends payments for other businesses is not offered yet.

Nothing to buy, plenty to see.

The demo runs every check on a made-up company in your browser. Nothing you type leaves the page.
