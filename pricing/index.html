Questions

Does Quarter take a cut of my revenue?

Quarter never holds or moves money, so it cannot take a cut of a payment. Pricing will be a plan you pay for separately.

What counts as a paying user?

We plan to count each of your users who had active access or a credit balance in the month, once.

Do test mode purchases count?

We do not plan to count them. Test mode is for building and testing.

Is anything billed today?

No. Quarter is pre-launch and bills nothing. Early access teams help set these prices.

Help set the launch price.

Early access teams run Quarter on their own paid apps before launch, and their volumes shape these plans.
