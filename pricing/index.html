On every plan

The safe path never costs more than the unsafe one.

Retries are free

One action is one business id, however many attempts, replays or lost-response checks it takes to settle.

Reworded retries are not billed

The same id with different input is refused before dispatch as ACTION_ID_CONFLICT, and never counts.

Approvals are included

Holding an action for a person to approve is the safe path, so it never costs extra.

The executor is never an upsell

Running provider calls in your own network, with your own credentials, is available on every plan.

Questions

What counts as a protected action?

A consequential write sent through Quarter with an ActionSpec, such as a refund, a cancel or an email. It counts once, when Quarter plans it, whatever happens after.

What is an observed call?

Metadata about a provider call your own code made, recorded by SDK capture or OTLP import. Observed calls feed call classes, drift and provider health, so they come as a generous allowance instead of a second bill.

What happens past the included actions on Free?

New actions are held for review with an alert, not refused. Refusing a refund your code already decided on would be a production incident.

Do simulated sandbox actions count?

They count toward the Free allowance, so you can try every fault in the Provider Lab without a card.

Is anything billed today?

No. Quarter is pre-launch and bills nothing. These are the prices we plan to launch with, and early access teams help set them.
