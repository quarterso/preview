Questions

Does Quarter take a cut of our payments?

No. Quarter never holds or moves money, so it cannot take a cut of a payment. Pricing will be a plan you pay for separately.

Is there a guarantee against losses?

No. Quarter does not guarantee that it will catch every fraudulent payment, and you decide every payment you release. Its records are the evidence your bank, auditor or insurer will ask for.

What does the scan cost?

Nothing. The 90-day payment scan is free, whatever you decide afterwards.

Is anything billed today?

No. Quarter is pre-launch and bills nothing. Our first customers will help set these prices.
