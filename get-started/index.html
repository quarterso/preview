Get started

Put a check between every payment and your bank.

Your team keeps paying the way it does now, and every payment is checked before it leaves.

What happens next

  1. Request accessTell us who you are and how your business sends payments.
  2. Set up your workspaceImport your vendor list or connect NetSuite, invite your approvers and choose which checks hold a payment.
  3. Confirm your vendorsSend each vendor a link to confirm their own bank details, or record a call-back to a number you already had.
  4. Check every runUpload each payment file before it goes to your bank, or send it through the API. Held payments wait for a person.

Quarter never holds or moves money. Account numbers are encrypted when stored, and only the last 4 digits are ever shown.

Questions

How do our vendors get into Quarter?

Import your vendor list as a CSV, or connect NetSuite and Quarter reads your vendors and their bank details every hour. The NetSuite connection is tested against a sample account; it has not yet run against a real one.

Do you need our bank logins?

No. Quarter never logs in to your bank and never sends anything to it. You upload the payment file, and you send the released file to your bank yourself.

Can we use it through our own software?

Yes. Vendors, payment runs, checks and webhooks are in the API, described in OpenAPI so any language can call it.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. Team starts with a 14-day free trial, and larger companies start with a 60-day paid pilot on their real payment runs that counts toward the first year.
