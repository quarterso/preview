Not open yet

Quarter is not open yet.

There is no sign-up, trial or waiting list. You can see everything that is built without giving us your name or email.

What is built today

  • 32 checks on bank files, check runs, wires, instant and international payments, card payments and stablecoin payments.
  • AI agents as payers, each with a per-payment and daily limit and a list of allowed payees.
  • Holds decided by named people, two-person approval and an audit log chained by hash.
  • A NetSuite connection, tested against a sample account and not yet run against a real one.

Quarter never holds or moves money.

Questions

Can we sign up or join a waiting list?

No. Quarter collects nothing from visitors while it is not open. There is no form on this site, and it sets no cookies.

Is anything running in production?

No. Everything listed here is built and tested, and nothing is deployed for customers yet.

Does Quarter hold or move money?

No, and it never will. It checks each payment and records the decision. Your bank, card issuer or wallet sends the money, as it does today.
