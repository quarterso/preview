Company

Know where every payment goes, before it goes.

Too many business payments leave on the word of an email, a spreadsheet and a phone call. We are building the check that should have been there all along.

A note from the founders

Why we are building Quarter

Business email compromise does not break into a bank. It persuades a careful person to send the money themselves. The tools most finance teams have against it are a spreadsheet, a phone call and hope.

We think it can be fixed. A supplier can prove their own bank account in a minute by logging in to their bank. A payment run can be checked line by line before it goes out. And a bank account confirmed by one business could count for every business that pays it.

So that is what we are building, starting with the check every payment run needs. We will always say plainly what is built and what is not. And we will never ask you to trust us with your money, because we never touch it.

[Founder names], founders of Quarter

Where this goes

Every business checks the same bank accounts alone. That is the real gap.

A supplier can be paid by hundreds of businesses. Today each of them confirms the same bank details on its own, and a scammer who fails at one simply tries the next. Quarter connects those checks, so every business learns from each one.

  1. Now

    The firewall

    Every payment a business sends is checked before it leaves the bank, with a person deciding anything that looks wrong.

  2. Next

    The network

    A bank account confirmed by one business counts for every business that pays it. An account reported in a fraud once is held for everyone.

  3. Then

    The release point

    Every outgoing payment passes through Quarter on its way to any bank, from any ERP, accounting tool or AP platform.

  4. The goal

    The trusted layer for business payments

    One place any business, platform or bank can ask "is this the right account?" before money moves.

We start with the firewall because it is useful to one business on its first day. The network grows with every customer that joins, and it only ever shares counts, never who.

Principles

How we make decisions.

A person decides

Quarter holds a payment and explains why. It never decides for you.

Plain words

Every hold gives its reason in a sentence an AP clerk can act on.

Never touch the money

No holding, moving or sending money, and nothing sent to your bank.

Counts, never who

The network tells you how many businesses vouched for an account. Never which.

Hold as little as possible

Account numbers sealed, the last 4 digits shown, no passwords anywhere.

Honest about what is built

Planned work is labelled planned, everywhere we write about it.

Focus

What we will not build.

Quarter is pre-launch. See what is live and what is planned.

  • A bank, a payment processor, or anything that holds or moves money
  • A service that sends files or instructions to your bank for you
  • A guarantee against losses. You decide every payment you release
  • A credit bureau. Network signals are never for credit, insurance, employment or housing decisions
  • A list that names who reported an account
