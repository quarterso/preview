Company

Know where every payment goes, before it goes.

Too many business payments leave on the word of an email, a spreadsheet and a phone call, and each way a business pays has its own rules. Quarter puts every payment under one set of rules, with a record of each decision.

A note from the founders

Why we are building Quarter

Business email compromise does not break into a bank. It persuades a careful person to send the money themselves. The tools many finance teams have against it are a spreadsheet, a phone call and hope.

We think it can be fixed. A supplier can prove their own bank account by logging in to their bank. A payment run can be checked line by line before it goes out. And every check, call-back and approval can leave a record that holds up when someone asks.

So that is what we are building, starting with businesses that pay vendors from their own bank. We will always say plainly what is built and what is not. And we will never ask you to trust us with your money, because we never touch it.

Where this goes

One set of rules over every way a business pays.

Bank files, wires, cards, stablecoins and AI agents each come with their own controls. Quarter puts one set of rules and one record over all of them, and never holds or moves the money. The NetSuite payment flow is tested against a simulated account and has not yet run against a real one.

  1. Built

    Bank payments

    Payment runs, check runs, wires, instant and international payments, and open NetSuite bills, each checked before it goes, with a person deciding anything that looks wrong.

  2. Built

    Cards, stablecoins and AI agents

    The same rules and the same record for a card or stablecoin payment, and for an AI agent paying within limits you set. In the API and the console, and for agents over MCP.

  3. Built

    In the books

    Each approved payment matched to what settled on the bank or card statement, and any money that left without Quarter flagged.

  4. As customers join

    The network

    An account one business confirmed or reported counts for the others, as a number and never a name. It is built, and it starts empty.

The checks are useful to one business on its first day. The network only helps once many businesses use Quarter, and it only ever shares counts, never who.

Principles

How we make decisions.

A person decides

Quarter holds a payment and explains why. It never decides for you.

Plain words

Every hold gives its reason in a sentence an AP clerk can act on.

Never touch the money

No holding, moving or sending money, and nothing sent to your bank, card issuer or wallet.

Counts, never who

The network tells you how many businesses vouched for an account. Never which.

Hold as little as possible

Account numbers sealed, the last 4 digits shown, no passwords anywhere.

Honest about what is built

Planned work is labelled planned, everywhere we write about it.

Focus

What we will not build.

Quarter is not open yet. See what is built and what is planned.

  • A bank, a payment processor, a card issuer, a wallet, or anything that holds or moves money
  • A service that sends files or instructions to your bank for you
  • A guarantee against losses. You decide every payment you release
  • A credit bureau. Network signals are never for credit, insurance, employment or housing decisions
  • A list that names who reported an account
