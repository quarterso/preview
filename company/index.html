Company

Payments that just work.

Every purchase should become the right access, exactly once. Today that takes careful code for every store, and most apps never get it fully right.

Thesis

Start with the payment that went wrong.

The first problem is the user who paid and is still locked out. Quarter checks every payment with the store, changes access once, and keeps it in step through renewals, cancels and refunds. Credits for AI apps come with it, spent atomically and never twice.

We start with people shipping paid apps built with AI tools, then any app that sells subscriptions or credits.

Quarter is pre-launch. See what is live and what is planned.

Principles

How we make decisions.

The store is the source of truth

Nothing unlocks until the store confirms it.

Exactly once

A payment seen twice changes access once.

Never touch the money

Payments stay in your own store accounts.

Hold as little as possible

Your user ids, store ids and amounts. Nothing more.

Honest about what is built

Planned work is labelled planned, everywhere we write about it.

Depth before breadth

Three stores done properly before the next one.

Focus

What we will not build.

  • A payment processor, or anything that holds or moves money
  • A merchant of record. Tax stays with Stripe, Apple, Google or you
  • A seller of credits. Your app sells them, Quarter keeps the ledger
  • Credits that can be cashed out or moved between apps
  • A store for your users' names, emails or card details
