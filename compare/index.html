Where Quarter fits next to what you already have.

Each description is what the product says about itself on its own pages. We say when another option is the better fit. Prices and features change, so check the source before you decide.

01Options

Seven options, read side by side.

OptionWhat it does, by its own descriptionThe better fit whenWhere Quarter differs

  1. 01Your bank's Positive Pay

    What it does, by its own description
    The bank compares each check presented with the issue file you sent it, and asks you about the ones that do not match.1
    The better fit when
    Always keep it. It catches altered and counterfeit checks at the bank, which Quarter cannot see.
    Where Quarter differs
    Works before the bank: it screens the check register before the checks are printed and writes the issue file in your bank’s layout. It also checks ACH payments and wires to vendors, which check Positive Pay does not cover.

  2. 02Payee name checks at your bank

    What it does, by its own description
    The Federal Reserve's Payee Name Verification lets a bank compare a payee name with the account's past FedACH payments.2
    The better fit when
    Use it where your bank offers it. It is evidence about the account.
    Where Quarter differs
    Adds the controls around the payment: holds on changed and unconfirmed details, two people, segregation of duties and the record. A brand-new account has no payment history to match.

  3. 03Ramp Bill Pay

    What it does, by its own description
    Reviews each AP transaction it processes against 60 signals and flags changed or unverified vendor bank details. Bill pay is on Ramp’s free plan, with per-payment fees.3,4
    The better fit when
    If you pay, or are ready to pay, your bills through Ramp.
    Where Quarter differs
    Checks the payments you already send from your own bank and NetSuite, without moving them to a payables platform.

  4. 04BILL

    What it does, by its own description
    Sends a $0.01 test deposit to confirm a vendor’s bank account can receive payments, for payments made through BILL.5
    The better fit when
    If you pay vendors through BILL.
    Where Quarter differs
    Never moves money, so it confirms details by the vendor’s own bank login or a recorded call-back instead of a deposit, for payments made outside BILL.

  5. 05Eftsure

    What it does, by its own description
    Verifies payee details against a large network, with segregation of duties and a guarantee of up to $1M on verified payments. Inside NetSuite it flags high-risk transactions and can block them by rule, and it can send validated files from NetSuite to the bank. It says most businesses are running within a few weeks.6,7,8
    The better fit when
    If you want a guarantee, a large verification network, international vendor coverage, or files sent to your bank for you.
    Where Quarter differs
    Offers no guarantee, and its network starts empty. It never sends anything to your bank. Built for a mid-market AP team that pays from its own bank: you connect NetSuite yourself, and Quarter holds risky bills with NetSuite's own Payment Hold box. Once open, it will start with a trial or a 60-day pilot.

  6. 06Trustpair

    What it does, by its own description
    Validates supplier bank accounts in 190 countries for large companies, with 20+ connectors and up to $1M of liability protection. Basware agreed to buy it in 2026.9,10
    The better fit when
    If you are a large company with suppliers in many countries.
    Where Quarter differs
    Focused on US companies paying by ACH, check and wire from their own bank, with check runs and Positive Pay files included.

  7. 07A written call-back policy

    What it does, by its own description
    Nacha's guidance for originators names out-of-band verification and dual control as controls, scaled to the size of the business.11
    The better fit when
    It is the control. Keep it, whatever tool you use.
    Where Quarter differs
    Holds the payment until the call is done, refuses a number taken from the change request, and records who called, which number and where it came from.

02Fair to say

What Quarter does not have yet.

If one of these is a must for you, another option above may suit you better today. See what is built and what is planned.

  • A guarantee or reimbursement of any loss
  • A verification network with scale. Quarter’s starts empty
  • A SOC 2 report or an outside penetration test
  • Customers. Quarter is not open yet
  • Connectors beyond NetSuite, and bank login with real banks until Plaid approves production
