Free 90-day payment scan

See who you paid without anyone confirming where the money went.

Send us last quarter's payment files. We run every payment through Quarter's thirteen checks and show you which payees were never verified, which bank accounts changed, and how much money went to them.

How it works

  1. Request your scanFill in the form. It asks nothing about your bank logins or account numbers.
  2. We replyWith a secure way to send your files, and the answer to anything you asked.
  3. You send the filesLast quarter's payment files as they went to the bank, and your vendor list if you have one.
  4. We walk you through itEvery finding, the reason for it, and the money it touched. Then you decide what to do.

Quarter never logs in to your bank and never sends anything to it. Account numbers are encrypted when stored, and the report shows only the last 4 digits.

Request your free scan

How do you send payments?

We use these details only to reply to you and to prepare your scan. No files are sent with this form.

What you get back

One report, in dollars and plain words.

  • Every payee, checked. Who you paid, how much, and whether their bank details were ever confirmed.
  • Changed accounts. Bank details that changed during the quarter, and what you paid to them afterwards.
  • Everything else that would have been held. Duplicates, first payments, unusual amounts, shared accounts and names that resemble the OFAC SDN list.
  • The money at risk. The total that went to accounts nobody had confirmed, in dollars.
  • A draft procedure. A written fraud-monitoring procedure for your settings, ready to adapt.

Questions

What do you need from us?

Last quarter's payment files, as they went to your bank: NACHA files or CSV exports. Your vendor list helps if you have it. About ten minutes of your time.

Do you need access to our bank or our ERP?

No. Just the files. Quarter never logs in to your bank and never sends anything to it.

What happens to our files?

We use them only to prepare your scan. Account numbers are encrypted when stored, and the report shows only the last 4 digits.

What does it cost?

Nothing. If the scan finds something worth fixing, we will offer a pilot on your live payment runs.

Is this a guarantee that we have no fraud?

No. The scan shows what Quarter would have held and why. You decide what to do about it.
