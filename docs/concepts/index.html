Concepts

Apps, products, entitlements, credits and customers, the statuses a purchase moves through, and the rules Quarter keeps.

The objects

ObjectId prefixWhat it is
Appapp_One product you sell, with its stores, its products and its users.
SourceA store connected to an app: stripe, app_store or play_store.
Productprod_A store product, such as a Stripe price or an App Store product id, and what it grants.
EntitlementA name your app checks, such as pro. A product can grant several.
CreditsA balance in a currency you name, such as credits or tokens. A user can hold several currencies.
Customercus_One of your users, known by your own user id. Quarter never needs a name or email.
Purchasegrant_One subscription or one-time purchase from a store, and the access it gives.
Eventevt_An append-only record of something that changed. Every event is also sent to your webhook endpoints.

Test and live

API keys start with qk_test_ or qk_live_, and each key belongs to a project in one environment. A test key never reads or writes live data, and the reverse. A test project takes only a Stripe rk_test_ key, and a live project only an rk_live_ key.

Your user ids

Every call names the user with your own id: the id in your auth system, or an anonymous id before sign-in. Quarter creates the customer the first time it sees a purchase for that id. When an anonymous user signs in, link the two ids and their purchases and credits move with them.

Products

KindWhat it isWhat it can grant
subscriptionRenews each period until cancelled.Entitlements, and credits each period.
non_consumableBought once, kept for good.Entitlements, credits, or both.
consumableBought again and again, such as a credit pack.Credits only.

A purchase of a store product you have not configured is still recorded, with unconfigured_product: true, and grants nothing. Configure every product before you sell it.

Purchase statuses

StatusGives accessMeaning
activeYesPaid for the current period, or a one-time purchase.
in_grace_periodYesA renewal payment failed, and the store's grace period has not ended.
in_billing_retryNoThe grace period ended and the store is still trying to collect.
pausedNoThe subscription is paused in the store.
expiredNoThe period ended without a renewal, for example after a cancel.
revokedNoRefunded or revoked by the store.

Status comes from the stored facts and the clock, so access ends on time even if the store never sends another notification. An entitlement is active while any purchase that grants it gives access.

The rules Quarter keeps

  • Nothing grants access unless the store confirmed it: Apple signed it, or Quarter fetched it from Google or Stripe.
  • Every store record is stored once and never changed. Access is always derived from the records.
  • A record older than the newest one applied for the same subscription never moves access backwards.
  • Applying the same facts twice changes nothing and sends no events.
  • A credit balance is the sum of its unexpired lots and is never negative. A spend with the same idempotency key happens once.
  • Credits bought on the App Store never expire, as Apple requires.
