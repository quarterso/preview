Bank change emails

Forward a vendor's email asking to change bank details. Quarter checks who really sent it and holds payments to the new details when it fails.

View as Markdown
Bank change email checks are built and tested. They have not yet been used by a customer. There is no inbound email address yet: send the email through the API.

Most vendor payment fraud starts with an email that asks you to pay a new account. Save that email from your mail client as a .eml file, headers and all, and send it to Quarter with the vendor it claims to come from. Quarter checks the sender, the email's authentication results, its wording and the bank details in it, and returns every finding.

An email never confirms bank details, however clean it looks. Uploading one can hold payments, never release them. Confirm a change with a verification link or a call-back to the number in your vendor record.

What Quarter checks

Each finding has a severity. A hold finding sets flagged: true, and payments to the bank details the email asked for are then held. A note is shown to the reviewer and holds nothing.

CodeSeverityWhen
sender_lookalikeholdThe sender's domain imitates the vendor's email_domain, found the same way as for the sender's domain.
sender_other_domainholdThe sender's domain is not the vendor's email_domain.
reply_to_elsewhereholdReplies go to a different domain from the sender's.
dmarc_failedholdDMARC failed.
spf_failedholdSPF failed.
return_path_elsewherenoteBounces go to a different domain from the sender's. Mailing services do this too.
first_time_sendernoteThe first email from this address uploaded to Quarter for the business.
authentication_missingnoteNo Authentication-Results header, so whether the sender is who it claims is unknown.
spf_weaknoteSPF result softfail, none or permerror.
dkim_failednoteDKIM result other than pass.
urgencynoteWording that presses for speed, such as urgent, immediately or final notice.
secrecynoteWording that asks for secrecy or to avoid a call, such as confidential, do not call or an old account said to be closed.
bank_details_differnoteThe bank details in the email differ from the ones on file.
account_newnoteThe account in the email has never been on file for any vendor of the business.

The domain checks need the vendor's email_domain. SPF, DKIM and DMARC come from the first Authentication-Results header, the one your own mail server added when the email arrived. Bank details are read from the body only when it gives a valid 9-digit routing number with an account number of 4 to 17 digits.

The hold on payments

Quarter links the email to the bank change it asked for: the bank_account_id you send, or else the vendor's bank details with the account the email gives. While those details are unconfirmed, a payment to them holds as request_email_suspicious, with each hold finding in the message. Once the vendor confirms the details by a verification link or a call-back, the check stops firing.

What is stored and what is not

  • Kept: the headers (up to 200, each up to 2,000 characters), the sender, Reply-To and Return-Path addresses, subject, Message-ID, date, authentication results and the findings.
  • Never kept: the body. Quarter reads it once to find the phrases and bank details, then keeps only the phrases it matched in matched_phrases.
  • Bank details in the body are kept as the routing number, the last 4 digits and a fingerprint to compare with other accounts, never the full account number.
  • Each upload is recorded in the audit log with the email, the bank change and the finding codes.
  • The list of emails leaves out the headers; retrieve one email to read them.

Endpoints

Upload a bank change email

POST/v1/bank_change_emailsAuth: API key

Checks the email and keeps it as evidence on the vendor. An agent's key answers 403 agent_key_refused.

Errors: 400 vendor_id_required, eml_required, eml_too_large, eml_invalid (no From header: upload the original, not a forward), bank_account_invalid; 404 vendor_not_found.

Body

FieldTypeRequiredDescription
vendor_idstringYesThe vendor the email claims to be from.
emlstringYesThe raw message as your mail client saves it, headers and all. At most 2 MB.
bank_account_idstringNoThe bank change the email asked for, one of this vendor's bank details.

List bank change emails

GET/v1/bank_change_emailsAuth: API key

Newest first, without their headers. Errors: 400 limit_invalid.

Query parameters

FieldTypeRequiredDescription
vendor_idstringNoOnly this vendor's emails.
limitintegerNo1 to 200, 50 by default.

Retrieve a bank change email

GET/v1/bank_change_emails/{email}Auth: API key

The email with its headers and findings. Unknown: 404 bank_change_email_not_found.

Path parameters

FieldTypeRequiredDescription
emailstringYesThe email id, starting with bce_.
