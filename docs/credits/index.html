Credits

Charge for what each AI call used with credit holds, spend credits atomically, and sell credits with a plan or as packs.

Credits are balances in currencies you name, such as credits or tokens. Your app sells them through the stores, and Quarter keeps the ledger. A balance can never go below zero, and a charge can never happen twice.

Charge for an AI call

You rarely know what an AI call costs until it ends. So hold the most it can cost before it runs, and when it ends, spend what it used. The rest goes back.

  • A failed call costs nothing: release the hold, and everything held goes back.
  • Parallel calls cannot overspend, because held credits leave the balance at once.
  • A retry with the same idempotency key holds once and charges once.
  • A hold that is never settled is released when it expires, 15 minutes by default.

When the balance cannot cover the hold, it fails with 409 insufficient_credits and nothing changes. See the Credits API for every field.

Spend a known amount

When the cost is fixed, spend it in one call. Send an idempotency_key from your own system, such as the id of the request. If the request is retried, Quarter answers with the same result and "replayed": true, and spends nothing more.

When the balance is too low, the spend fails with 409 insufficient_credits and nothing changes. Amounts can have up to six decimals.

Where credits come from

  • A subscription product can add credits every period. With expires: "period_end" they expire when the period ends, and with "never" they stay.
  • A one-time product, such as a credit pack, adds credits once per purchase.
  • Your backend can grant credits directly, for a bonus or a support gesture.
  • Credits bought through the App Store never expire, because Apple requires that in-app purchases of credits do not.

Each grant is a separate lot with its own expiry. Spends and holds take from the lot that expires soonest, so users never lose credits they could have used.

Refunds

When a purchase is fully refunded or voided, Quarter takes back the credits it added that are still unspent, and sends credits.clawed_back. Credits already spent stay spent, and the balance never goes below zero.

Events

Every change to a balance is an event: credits.granted, credits.spent, credits.expired, credits.clawed_back, credits.reserved and credits.released. See Webhooks.
