Get started

Quarter docs

Turn every Stripe, App Store and Google Play payment in your app into the right access, exactly once, and spend credits atomically.

Your app sells a plan or credits through Stripe on the web, the App Store or Google Play. Quarter checks each payment with the store, unlocks the plan or adds the credits once, and takes access away on cancel, refund or a failed renewal. Your app asks one question: what does this user have right now?

What you build

  1. Connect your stores: Stripe, the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Say what each store product grants, with products: an entitlement such as pro, credits, or both.
  3. Report purchases from your app with POST /v1/apps/{app}/purchases.
  4. Ask what a user has with GET /v1/apps/{app}/customers/{user}/access, or read the mirror table in your own database.
  5. If you sell credits for AI calls, hold them while each call runs and spend what it used. A failed call costs nothing.

Start here

  • Quickstart: a Stripe test payment to an unlocked plan and a credit spend.
  • Concepts: apps, products, entitlements, credits, customers and statuses.
  • Webhooks: event types, signature verification and retries.
  • TypeScript SDK: the same calls from your backend, with last known access during an outage.
  • Building with an AI tool? Give it /llms-full.txt, the whole of these docs in one Markdown file. The exact API contract is GET /openapi.json on your API URL.

What Quarter never does

  • It never holds or moves money. Payments stay in your own Stripe, Apple or Google account.
  • It never sees card data.
  • It uses only a read-only Stripe restricted key.
  • It stores only your own user ids, not names or emails.

Status

Quarter is pre-launch. Stripe, App Store and Google Play purchases, access, credits with holds, the mirror, webhooks, erasure and export are built and tested against simulated stores. The TypeScript SDK is built and coming to npm. The console and self-serve sign-up are planned. During early access, Quarter issues your API keys and your API URL.
